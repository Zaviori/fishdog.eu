Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

fishdog is a personal, non-commercial bot that streams and uploads Action Quake 2 match footage to its operator's own YouTube channel.

What Google data it uses

fishdog uses the YouTube Data API through YouTube API Services, with the operator's own Google account only. With that access it creates and ends live broadcasts, sets their titles, descriptions, chapters and thumbnails, uploads highlight videos, and adds them to playlists on that channel. It does not access any other Google account, and it does not read, collect or store data about YouTube viewers.

What is stored

The OAuth refresh token is stored on the operator's own computer and is never shared. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Game data

The videos and match statistics come from public game servers and their public demo recordings, and show the in-game nicknames players chose themselves.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

By using fishdog's videos and streams you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
